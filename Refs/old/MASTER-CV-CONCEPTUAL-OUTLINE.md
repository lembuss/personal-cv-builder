# Phase 6 — Master CV Blueprint v1

The Master CV should be the **comprehensive professional source document**, not a recruiter-optimized two-page CV. Its job is to capture the strongest version of your professional identity so that we can later derive the five targeted CVs without repeatedly going back to the database.

## 1. Header

**Purpose:** Immediate professional identification.

**Contents:**

* Brian Lembuss
* **Aircraft Systems Engineer | Aerospace R&D | UAS & Autonomous Systems**
* Nairobi, Kenya
* Open to Europe / international opportunities
* Email
* Phone
* LinkedIn
* Portfolio/GitHub where useful

**No:** "Founder & CEO" in the headline. Kipepeo belongs in Experience.

---

## 2. Professional Profile

**Target:** ~100–130 words.

This is where Phase 5's narrative becomes a **CV profile**, not prose.

It should establish, in this order:

1. Aircraft systems engineer identity
2. End-to-end lifecycle capability
3. Manned + unmanned aircraft exposure
4. Hardware/software integration
5. R&D/eVTOL/UAS experience
6. Current geographic/role direction

**Important:** No childhood story, PPL story, motivational language or claims such as "rare at this career stage."

### Within each experience

We don't dump every activity.

Instead:

**Experience → selected projects → strongest activities/evidence**

Each role should demonstrate **progression and engineering ownership**.

For example, Kipepeo should not become 15 bullets describing every AngaStack feature. We select the evidence that demonstrates:

> requirements → architecture → hardware → software → integration → field deployment → system ownership.

# The weighting

The Master CV should roughly feel like:

**Professional identity + competencies**
↓
**Professional engineering experience** ← biggest weight
↓
**Technical/R&D projects**
↓
**Education / research**
↓
**Teaching / licences / technical skills**

Not:

> Education → skills → certificates → everything else.

That's important because **your professional experience is now strong enough to lead the document.**

---

# What the Master CV deliberately does NOT try to do

It will **not**:

* tell your entire life story;
* reproduce the Professional Narrative;
* include every database activity;
* list every university project;
* showcase every leadership role;
* optimise itself for one job description;
* pretend the MSc is completed;
* make Kipepeo dominate the document simply because it is your company;
* turn you into a generic "full-stack aerospace/software engineer."

---

## The core design principle

The reader should finish the first half of the CV with one clear conclusion:

> **Brian is an aircraft systems engineer who can work across the complete system lifecycle and unusually comfortably across the hardware/software boundary, with substantial R&D and UAS/eVTOL experience.**

Everything else in the Master CV should **support that conclusion**.
