# Phase 6 — Master CV Blueprint (V2 — widened scope)

## What changed from V1

V1's evidence-hierarchy table and exclusion list were built for a five-variant, industry-anchored plan (Aircraft Systems / UAS-Autonomy / Aerospace R&D / Academic-Research / Scholarship). The scope has since widened to include Entrepreneurial and Further-Education tracks. Two things needed fixing:

1. The evidence-hierarchy table (below) had no column for Entrepreneurial or Further-Education relevance — venture-building evidence had nowhere to register as high-priority.
2. The exclusion list explicitly named **"irrelevant university leadership"** as something the Master CV should drop. Per the Phase 6B V3 re-review, that leadership evidence isn't irrelevant — it's relevant to the TA/Academic and Further-Education tracks specifically. It stays excluded from the *industry* Master CV by default, but is no longer written off as unimportant.

Everything else in V1 (page structure, section order, weighting principle, page 1–4 layout) is unchanged and still governs the default (industry-anchored) Master CV.

# What gets excluded from the (default, industry-anchored) Master CV — corrected

Even though it exists in the database:

* Generic soft skills
* Generic career objectives
* Every student project
* Every activity
* Minor training courses
* ~~Irrelevant university leadership~~ **University leadership roles (VP Aerospace Society, Secretary General ISA, MUN President)** — excluded from the *industry* Master CV by default because they don't strengthen the aircraft-systems technical case, **not** because they lack value. They are CORE/SUPPORTING evidence for the TA/Academic and Further-Education variants — see Phase 6B V3.
* Duplicated technical evidence
* Claims unsupported by the database
* Narrative biography
* Self-congratulatory comparative claims
* Anything included merely to increase CV length

**The database remains comprehensive; the default Master CV remains selective — but "excluded from the default" no longer means "excluded everywhere."**

# The Master CV → Target variant architecture (updated)

The Master CV remains the industry-anchored parent document (per the roadmap's Option A decision: one broad Master CV, tailored variants downstream). Seven variants now draw from it:

1. Aircraft Systems
2. UAS / Autonomy
3. Aerospace R&D
4. Academic / Research
5. Scholarship / MSc completion
6. **Entrepreneurial / accelerator-incubator**
7. **Further education / university admission**

Variants 6 and 7 draw more heavily on evidence that sits outside the default Master CV's page budget (venture-building, leadership) — per Phase 6B V3 §5, these have their own track-specific writing pools rather than being compressed into the industry-anchored document.

## The principle governing the whole thing (extended)

**The database contains Brian's career.**
**The competency map tells us what is strongest — across all tracks.**
**The narrative tells us how the career coheres.**
**The Master CV decides what deserves space in the default, industry-anchored document.**
**Phase 6B V3 decides what deserves space in each of the other six tracks.**
**The targeted variants decide what deserves emphasis.**
