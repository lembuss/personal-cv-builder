# Phase 6 — Master CV Blueprint (Consolidated Multi-Track Version)

**Purpose:** A comprehensive, multi-track source CV blueprint from which seven targeted CV variants will be derived.
**Target length:** 3–4 pages initially (letting the evidence determine the final length).

---

## PAGE 1 — PROFESSIONAL IDENTITY

### 1. Header
**Fields:**
* Name
* Professional headline
* Location
* Email
* Phone
* LinkedIn
* Portfolio/GitHub where relevant

**Headline direction:**
> **Aircraft Systems Engineer | Aerospace R&D | UAS & Aircraft Integration**
*(Exact headline to be finalized from the database evidence before drafting).*

### 2. Professional Profile
**~100–130 words**
Purpose: Establish the professional identity immediately.
**Structure:**
1. Aircraft systems engineering identity
2. End-to-end systems lifecycle
3. Manned + unmanned aircraft experience
4. Hardware/software integration
5. R&D / experimental aerospace
6. Current target direction

### 3. Core Competencies
**~12–16 competencies maximum**, grouped by importance.
**Primary competency group:**
* Aircraft Systems Engineering
* Systems Engineering
* Systems Integration
* Requirements & Architecture
* Verification & Validation
* Avionics / Embedded Systems
* UAS Development
**Secondary/specialist group:** Derived from competency map.
*(No proficiency bars, percentages, stars or self-assessed ratings).*

---

## PAGE 2 — PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE

### 4. Professional Experience
*This is the dominant section of the CV.*
**For each Experience:**
* **Experience header:** Organisation, Role/title, Location, Dates.
* **Experience summary:** 2–3 lines maximum, only where useful to establish context.
* **Selected evidence:** 3–6 bullets, selected from the database.
  Each bullet must demonstrate one or more of: engineering responsibility, technical complexity, lifecycle ownership, integration, verification/testing, R&D, or measurable outcome.
* **Selection principle:** We do not reproduce every database activity. We select the activities that collectively communicate the strongest version of the experience.

### 5. Selected Technical/R&D Projects
*This section is not a duplicate of Professional Experience. It exists for substantial technical work whose significance would otherwise be buried inside academic/student/R&D experiences.*
**For each selected project:**
* Project name
* Organisation/context
* Date
* 2–4 evidence bullets

---

## PAGE 3 — EDUCATION + RESEARCH

### 6. Education
* **TU Munich:** Degree/programme, Dates, Completion status (ECTS completed), relevant specialization/coursework, thesis status. Treatment must be factual, transparent, and compact.
* **METU:** Degree, dates, honours/result where documented, relevant academic focus.

### 7. Research & Publications
*Separate from Education.*
**Include:** Publications, research projects with meaningful outputs, conference/research contributions.
**Priority:** Evidence with an identifiable technical/research contribution.

### 8. Teaching & Academic Experience
**Scope:** Draws on a confirmed five-item teaching evidence base (SI-PASS, ASE301 TA, TUM IFR Simulator Instructor/Examiner, VP Aerospace Society's technical training sessions, STEM Mentorship).
**Treatment:** Each entry gets 1–3 bullets depending on significance. Enough for a genuine academic-service section in the TA/Academic variant, rather than a compact filler line.

---

## PAGE 4 — TECHNICAL CREDENTIALS

### 9. Licences & Certifications
Ordered by relevance: 
1. Aviation licences 
2. UAS qualifications 
3. Engineering/professional certifications 
4. Other relevant documented qualifications (No filler certifications).

### 10. Technical Skills
Grouped logically:
* **Programming / Software:** Only technologies actually supported by the database.
* **Engineering / Simulation:** CAD, CFD, numerical tools, modelling, etc.
* **Aerospace / UAS:** Avionics, flight-control platforms, GCS, HIL, etc.
* **Cloud / Software Systems:** Relevant software/cloud technologies from the database.

### 11. Languages
Only documented language proficiency.

---

## EXCLUSION RULES (The Selective Master CV)

Even though it exists in the database, the following gets **excluded from the default industry-anchored Master CV**:
* Generic soft skills & career objectives
* Every student project and activity (only highest impact selected)
* Minor training courses
* Duplicated technical evidence
* Claims unsupported by the database
* Narrative biography & self-congratulatory comparative claims
* Anything included merely to increase CV length

**Exception Note for University Leadership:** 
University leadership roles (VP Aerospace Society, Secretary General ISA, MUN President) are excluded from the *industry* Master CV by default because they do not strengthen the aircraft-systems technical case. However, they are **not** discarded entirely; they are CORE/SUPPORTING evidence specifically reserved for the TA/Academic and Further-Education variants.

## ARCHITECTURE & PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

**The Master CV → Target Variant Architecture:**
The Master CV remains the industry-anchored parent document. Seven variants draw from it:
1. Aircraft Systems
2. UAS / Autonomy
3. Aerospace R&D
4. Academic / Research
5. Scholarship / MSc completion
6. **Entrepreneurial / accelerator-incubator** (Draws heavily on track-specific venture-building pool)
7. **Further education / university admission** (Draws heavily on track-specific leadership/teaching pool)

**Production Workflow:**
1. Phase 6A — Evidence Mapping
2. Phase 6B — Content Selection (V4 multi-track)
3. Phase 6C — Draft Master CV
4. Phase 6D — Evidence Audit
5. Phase 6E — Review
6. Phase 6F — Master CV V1 Lock
7. Derive the seven targeted variants.

**Governing Principles:**
* The database contains the career.
* The competency map tells us what is strongest.
* The narrative tells us how the career coheres.
* The Master CV decides what deserves space in the default, industry-anchored document.
* Phase 6B (V4) decides what deserves space in each of the other six tracks.
* The targeted variants decide what deserves emphasis.
