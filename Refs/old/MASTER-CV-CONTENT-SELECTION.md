# MASTER CV — CONTENT SELECTION

## Purpose

This is the Phase 6B selection layer between the Evidence Map and the written Master CV. It applies professional judgement to the documented career evidence and determines what should receive space in the comprehensive Master CV.

**Source of truth:** `dev.db` and the Phase 6A Evidence Map. No new career facts are introduced.

### Selection labels
- **CORE** — should receive space in the Master CV.
- **SUPPORTING** — useful evidence; include where it strengthens an Experience or section without creating redundancy.
- **RESERVE** — retain for targeted CVs or later tailoring; not necessary for the first Master CV.
- **EXCLUDE** — not selected for the Master CV, while remaining part of the career database.

These labels concern Master CV space, **not the importance of the work to Brian's career**.
