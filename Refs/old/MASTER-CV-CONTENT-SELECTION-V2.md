# PHASE 6B — MASTER CV CONTENT SELECTION

## Purpose

Phase 6A mapped the career database to potential CV destinations. **Phase 6B is the professional judgement layer:** it decides what deserves space in the Master CV, what supports the main case, and what should remain available for targeted variants.

**Source of truth:** the career database (`dev(2).db`) and the approved Phase 5 positioning/blueprint. No new career facts have been introduced.

### Decision labels
- **CORE** — should be represented in the Master CV.
- **SUPPORTING** — useful evidence, but secondary or partly redundant with stronger evidence.
- **RESERVE** — retain for targeted CVs (academic, research, UAS operations, entrepreneurship, specialist roles), but do not spend Master CV space on it by default.

**Important distinction:** a RESERVE item is not unimportant career evidence. It simply does not earn scarce space in this particular Master CV.
