# PHASE 6B — MASTER CV CONTENT SELECTION (V4 — complete, multi-track)

## Purpose

This is the complete, standalone Phase 6B document. It replaces V1 and V2 — you should not need either open alongside this one. It supersedes the V3 addendum by folding V3's additions directly into the full V2 structure rather than referencing it[cite: 1].

Phase 6A mapped the career database to potential CV destinations[cite: 1]. **Phase 6B is the professional judgement layer:** it decides what deserves space in each of five application tracks — Industry, Entrepreneurial, TA/Academic, Scholarship, and Further Education — and what should remain in reserve[cite: 1].

**Source of truth:** the career database (`dev.db`)[cite: 1]. No new career facts have been introduced anywhere in this document[cite: 1].

### Decision labels
* **CORE** — should be represented in that track's CV/materials.
* **SUPPORTING** — useful evidence, but secondary or partly redundant.
* **RESERVE** — retain in the database for that track, but do not spend default space on it.
* **—** — no meaningful connection to that track.

The **Industry** column is the original V2 professional judgement, preserved exactly except where noted as reclassified. The four other columns are new. A RESERVE-for-Industry item is not unimportant career evidence — it may be CORE for a different track. Two corrections were necessary while adding the new columns, not just additions: the three METU leadership experiences were previously labelled RESERVE as "duplicate/secondary METU student record" — the same reasoning used for genuinely duplicate academic records, which wrongly buried real leadership evidence[cite: 1]; and the Kipepeo venture-building and Jasiri4Africa evidence was flat RESERVE with no activity-level detail anywhere in V2, despite being some of the strongest evidence in the database for the Entrepreneurial track[cite: 1]. Both are corrected below.
