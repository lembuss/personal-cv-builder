# 4. Master CV philosophy

The Master CV is **not** intended to contain every career activity with equal prominence, and it is **not** intended to be five documents stitched together.

Its purpose is to be the comprehensive professional source — now covering all five tracks' worth of evidence — from which targeted CVs/materials can be generated.

The important distinction established during the work, unchanged:

> **Important career evidence ≠ Master CV evidence ≠ individual CV bullet.**

An activity may be important and remain in the database without deserving a bullet in the Master CV. Likewise, a project may be CORE while only a handful of its activities should survive into actual CV writing.

Under the widened scope, add:

> **RESERVE-for-industry ≠ RESERVE-for-every-track.** An item can be correctly relegated for the industry-facing Master CV and still be essential evidence for the entrepreneurial or academic variant.

The Master CV should therefore:

- prioritise strong aircraft/UAS systems evidence (still the anchor)
- preserve R&D/deep-tech evidence
- demonstrate end-to-end systems lifecycle capability
- show genuine hardware/software integration
- retain verification/testing evidence
- preserve applied research evidence
- **retain venture-building evidence with clear track-tagging (not suppress it entirely as "administration")**
- **retain teaching/leadership evidence with clear track-tagging**
- avoid drowning the technical narrative in generic business/administrative activity **in the default/industry Master CV**, while not losing that evidence for tracks where it matters
- retain reserve evidence for later targeted variants
